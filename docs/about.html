About the maker

Mohammed Bremoo

@Moha-Bremoo

I made Bremorph, a free Mac app that converts, compresses and edits files right from Finder.

The project

One small app for everyday file chores.

Bremorph lives in the menu bar. You drag a file, hold a key, and drop it on the format or tool you want. The new file lands next to the original.

Everything runs on your Mac. The app has no network code, so your files never get uploaded anywhere.

It's open source under the MIT license, and the whole interface works in English and Arabic, with a proper right-to-left layout.
